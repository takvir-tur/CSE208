🔧 Three small additions to hash.cpp that half these problems lean on

Your chainHash/openHash classes only expose insert, search (which returns a probe count, not a value) and remove. A lot of the problems below need to actually look up a value or list everything stored — so add these once, near the bottom of chainHash, before you touch anything else:

  • bool contains(K key) — true/false membership test, for set problems.
  • bool find(K key, V& outValue) — the lookup your search() doesn't give you.
  • vector<pair<K,V>> getAll() — walks every bucket, needed to print/iterate the whole table.
  • void increment(K key) — insert-or-bump-by-1, for frequency counting.
Add to chainHash, right before the closing brace of the class
Gotcha worth knowing before problems 1 & 2: your nextSize() already multiplies its input by 2 internally (i = currSize*2+1), and prevSize() already halves its input (i = currSize/2-1). Your existing resize(nextSize(tableSize*2+1)) calls therefore grow the table ~4×, not ~2×, and resize(prevSize(tableSize/2)) shrinks it to ~¼, not ~½. Whenever a problem explicitly asks for "approximately double" or "approximately half", call nextSize(tableSize) and prevSize(tableSize) directly — not the doubled/halved argument.
